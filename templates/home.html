About Me

An aspiring developer who enjoys building websites and systems that help businesses automate and streamline their operations.



Hey! I'm Sheikh. I've been interested in coding since college, and after spending a few years working in sales and customer service, I've found my way back to something I've always enjoyed building things with technology.
